Controls & physics

Select and drag a peg along its mounting face to move its entire stack. Drag a wall along the floor to move its pegs too. Right-drag or Shift-drag orbits. Wheel zooms. Delete removes the selection; Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo/redo; Space runs or pauses.

Physical tooth contacts transmit motion. Cranks apply limited hand torque. Material failure approximates tooth and rim fracture; arbitrary crack propagation is not modeled. Save stores the intact mechanism. Reset repairs runtime damage.
