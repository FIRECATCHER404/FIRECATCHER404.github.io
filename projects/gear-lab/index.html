Controls & physics

Select and drag a peg along its mounting face to move its entire stack. Drag a wall along the floor to move its pegs too. Shift-click toggles parts in the selection. Add selection does the same on touch screens; the Parts list can reach covered gears. Ctrl+A selects all gears. Drag a selected part to move all selected mounts. Left-drag empty space to rotate while keeping the grabbed scene point under the pointer. The grabbed point stays under the cursor and the horizon stays level. Right-drag pans. Scroll moves forward or backward along the viewing direction. On touch screens, drag empty space with one finger to rotate; use two fingers to pan and pinch to move forward or back. Delete removes the selection; Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo/redo; Space runs or pauses.

Physical tooth contacts transmit motion. Cranks apply limited hand torque. Material failure approximates tooth and rim fracture; arbitrary crack propagation is not modeled. Save stores the intact mechanism. Reset repairs runtime damage.
