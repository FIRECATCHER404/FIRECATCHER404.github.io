Controls & physics

Click a tool, then the board. Select and drag a peg to move its entire stack. Right-drag or Shift-drag empty space to orbit. Wheel zooms. Delete removes the selection. Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo/redo. Space runs or pauses.

Rapier rigid-body contacts transfer motion through involute teeth. Material failure uses a beam-stress and accumulated damage approximation. Teeth and rim sectors detach as physical fragments. This is a sandbox, not engineering fracture analysis. Save design stores the intact mechanism; Reset repairs runtime damage.
