Controls and map files

Right-drag to pan. Scroll to zoom around the pointer. Two fingers pan and pinch on touch screens.

Place a pattern with a click or tap. Cell and erase tools support dragging. Space toggles running when the map is focused. Escape closes menus; R rotates Life patterns.

ZIP files preserve the map, step count, view, theme and speed. Open them on the same simulator page. A local draft is saved while you work.

On an off cell an ant turns right; on an on cell it turns left. It flips that cell and moves one cell. Multiple ants read the previous state simultaneously; an even number of flips on a shared cell cancel. Ants have no facing triangle or body. Position markers are optional.
