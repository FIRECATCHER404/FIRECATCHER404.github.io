Install and play

Download the JAR for your exact Minecraft version. Put it in your instance's mods folder alongside Fabric API, using Fabric Loader.

Press K in Minecraft to choose a skin pack. Packs made in Skin Studio work with both versions. To share animations, the server and other players also need Expressionful for their Minecraft version.
