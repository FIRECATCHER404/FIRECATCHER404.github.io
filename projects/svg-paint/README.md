# SVG Paint

A buildless vector editor with a Paint-style file bar and ribbon, a canvas, a shape inspector, layers, and flat light/dark themes. New drawings contain no supplied graphics. Artwork, drafts, and project files stay on the device.

- The shape gallery has 21 shapes: rectangle, ellipse, triangle, right triangle, diamond, parallelogram, trapezoid, pentagon, hexagon, octagon, regular polygon, star, burst, arrow, double arrow, chevron, heart, cross, ring, arc, and line. Drag to place one. Hold Shift to constrain proportions or line angles.
- Regular polygons, stars, bursts, and rings show **New shape options** before drawing: side/point count and inner size. These settings apply to the next shape; existing outlines remain editable with Points and the shape inspector. Ring holes remain separate closed contours in exported SVGs.
- Polygon: click its corners, then press Enter, double-click, or choose Finish polygon. Escape cancels the unfinished outline.
- Pencil: draw a freehand line. Redundant points are simplified on release.
- Select: drag a box over visible shapes, click a shape, or Shift/Ctrl-click to add or remove it. Box selection includes shapes whose bounding boxes intersect the marquee. Shift-drag a new box to add to the current selection. **Add selection** provides the same toggle/add behavior for touch. **Select all** / Ctrl+A selects visible shapes; Escape clears the selection or cancels an active drag.
- Drag any selected shape to move the whole selection. The shared corner handles resize shapes and their spacing together; the round handle rotates them around a shared center. Shift constrains movement, preserves proportions during resize, or snaps handle rotation to 15-degree increments. The inspector also sets exact group position, size, and rotation.
- Fill, outline, opacity, corner rounding, closed/open state, simplification, flip, duplicate, hide, delete, and layer-order actions apply to every selected shape. Mixed values are marked **Mixed** instead of silently replacing each shape's style. Undo/redo restores both the artwork and its selection; a group drag or slider drag is one undo step. Point editing is available with one shape selected.
- Points: drag individual points or set their X/Y values. Double-click an edge to insert a point; Delete removes the selected point. Corner rounding cuts back along adjacent edges and adds a quadratic curve, limited to half the length of either edge.
- Simplify threshold removes points within the chosen distance while retaining endpoints. Minimum outlines retain two points (open) or three (closed).
- The ribbon has separate **Fill** and **Outline** groups. Toggle either paint, use a color picker or 3/6-digit hex value, and set their opacities independently. The palette can target fill or outline, and Swap colors exchanges the two colors. Outline width has a precise 0–512 px field and a live slider; dragging the slider is one undo step, and Escape cancels the drag. Outline ends can be round, flat, or square; corners can be round, sharp, or beveled.
- With shapes selected, appearance controls edit the selection. Choosing a drawing tool switches them to **New shapes**, using the previous selection's appearance without changing it. Align the selection as a unit to the canvas, or choose **Shapes within selection** to align their individual edges/centers to each other. **Distribute X/Y** equalizes the gaps between three or more selected shapes while keeping the extreme shapes fixed. Canvas dimensions and an optional exported background are editable.

**Merge shapes** performs a vector union on two or more filled, closed shapes, removing overlapping/internal edges. Disconnected pieces and holes stay in one editable object. Open lines and arcs can instead use **Combine paths**, which joins outlines without removing overlaps. Both operations use the topmost selected shape's appearance and layer position; use ordinary multi-selection when you want to keep different colors and styles. Rounded quadratic edges are baked to vector segments within 0.05 drawing units, independent of display zoom. Very complex self-crossing compound paths can need cleanup before merging. Undo restores the original separate shapes. Boolean operations use the locally bundled, MIT-licensed [polygon-clipping](vendor/NOTICE.md).

Export SVG creates plain vector paths that can be imported into Shape Morph. Save/Open project preserves editable point geometry in `.svgpaint` files. A local draft restores after reload; theme choice is saved separately. Import SVG replaces the current drawing (Undo restores it) and samples curves into editable points. It supports the same plain geometry as [Shape Morph](../shape-morph/README.md); complex effects and text must be converted to solid-color outlines first. Transparency exports even though the working paper is displayed white.

Keyboard: Ctrl+A selects visible shapes, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z (or Ctrl+Y), Ctrl+D duplicates the selection, Ctrl+S saves a project, Delete removes selected shapes (or a selected point), arrows nudge the selection/point (Shift for 10 px), Escape cancels an active gesture or clears the selection. Ctrl+wheel zooms; Fit restores a useful canvas view. Mouse and touch gestures are supported; point insertion by double-click is easiest with a mouse.

Limits: 1–4096 px artboards, 64 shapes, 32,768 total points, 4,096 points per outline, 60 undo snapshots with a 4 MB history budget. Add a canvas background only when the combined exported shape count will stay within Shape Morph's 64-shape limit. Imports sample each outline to 128 points and flatten group opacity; imported curves are approximations, not editable Bezier handles. There are no text, image, gradient, subtraction/intersection, or persistent nested-group tools yet.

Run `node scripts/test-svg-paint.mjs`. To package a standalone editor: `python scripts/package-shape-morph.py --app svg-paint --output C:/path/SVG-Paint.html`. Keep it beside `Shape-Morph.html` for their cross-links to work offline.
