Logic Studio for Windows

The desktop edition includes the browser canvas tools with a compiled C++ circuit engine and native Direct2D drawing. Simulation and saving run separately to keep the editor responsive. It is the main version for new features.

The new RGB-16 computer has 128 KiB of RAM, 128 KiB of program ROM, and a 128×128 RGB565 screen. Load assembly, hex words or binary programs, inspect memory, and step through its gate-built CPU. The older 8-bit computers and Langton’s ant are included.
