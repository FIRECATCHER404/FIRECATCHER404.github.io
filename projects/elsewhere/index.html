A SMALL GUIDE TO ELSEWHERE

Architecture without
the usual rules.

Each room lives in its own space. Portals are live surfaces attached to walls: look through, walk across, and the next room takes over without a loading screen.

Build a contradiction

Add a portal, choose its wall and shape, then connect it to another surface. “Bigger inside” builds a large room behind a small opening. Link different walls of the same room for a looping shortcut.

Fit it like fabric

Choose Tarp and drag its four corner handles in the wall view. Width, height, position, and “Stretch to wall” change the whole surface.

Take a walk

WASD or arrow keys to move, drag to look. Shift to hurry. Capture mouse for free look. Enable Fly and use Q / E to descend or rise through higher windows.

Keep your bearings

Tab returns to Build. Ctrl Z undoes an edit. The graph shows links, not physical positions. Save a building file to keep or share it; a draft also stays in this browser.

Doorways preserve your view direction; differently sized ends can change your scale. Walkable scale is bounded to 0.15×–5×. Steps are decorative solid objects; use Fly to explore elevated pieces. Portals attach to the four upright walls.
